Fresher FAQs

Are laptops mandatory for first year?
Not necessarily from day one, but a laptop can be useful for programming assignments, projects, presentations and academic work.
Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?
Hostel accommodation depends on availability and the university's allotment process. Students should apply as early as possible.
How are the hostel facilities?
Hostel facilities are generally basic. Freshers should be prepared for a simple residential setup and should keep their essential personal items.
How are the engineering labs?
Engineering labs provide the facilities required for regular academic practical work. The experience can vary depending on the department and laboratory.
How are the professors?
Faculty members can be approached for academic guidance, assignments and doubts. Students are encouraged to interact with their department faculty.
What is the university's status?
Gurukul Kangri is a Deemed-to-be University.
Is there a dress code?
Students should follow university rules regarding appropriate clothing, particularly during formal academic activities and events.
