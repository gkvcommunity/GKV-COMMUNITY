Fresher FAQ

Quick answers to common questions
🔍
Showing all FAQs
Are laptops mandatory for 1st year?
A laptop can be useful for coding assignments, projects and academic work. A decent mid-range laptop should be enough for most introductory engineering work.
Is a hostel room guaranteed for freshers?
Hostel availability depends on the university's allotment process and available seats. Check the latest instructions and apply as early as possible.
How are the hostel facilities?
Hostel facilities are generally basic. Students should keep their personal essentials and supplies ready.
How are the classrooms and practical labs?
Engineering classrooms and laboratories provide facilities required for regular academic work. Facilities can vary between departments.
How are the professors and teaching staff?
Faculty members can help students with academic questions and subject-related guidance. Make use of faculty interaction and department resources.
What is the status of the university?
Gurukul Kangri is a Deemed-to-be University.
Is there a dress code?
Gurukul Kangri has a traditional academic environment. Students should follow the university's current dress and conduct guidelines.
Where can I find the engineering syllabus?
Use the Engineering Syllabus section above or the Important Resources section at the top of the page.
Where can I find the 2025 PYQs?
The 2025 Previous Year Questions are available in the dedicated 2025 PYQ section above.
